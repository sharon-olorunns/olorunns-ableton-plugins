OLORUNNS PLUGINS FOR ABLETON LIVE
=================================

Halo   - huge reverb with a freeze button and shimmer
Glitch - stutters, reverses and tape stops, played from your MIDI keyboard
Prism  - sing one note, hold a chord, and your voice sings the chord
Rig    - guitar amp and tone shaper with six styles


INSTALL
-------
1. Open Terminal (press Cmd+Space, type "Terminal", press Enter).
2. Paste this line and press Enter:

       bash ~/Downloads/Olorunns-Plugins-macOS/install.sh

   (If you unzipped somewhere else, drag install.sh onto the Terminal window
   after typing "bash " instead.)

3. In Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > turn on
   "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", then click "Rescan".

The plugins show up in Ableton's browser under Plug-Ins > Olorunns.

To update later, download the new zip and run the same line again.

More help: https://github.com/sharon-olorunns/olorunns-ableton-plugins
